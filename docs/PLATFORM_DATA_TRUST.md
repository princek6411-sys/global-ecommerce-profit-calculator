# Platform Data Trust Policy

## Verification states

### Verified
Supported by the supplied official-source research report.

### Unavailable
The platform or the consulted official source does not publish the required value.

### Unclassified
The available evidence is insufficient to classify the value or feature safely.

### Stale
The information is older than the configured freshness policy or its effective period has expired.

### Unverified
The supplied research did not establish the fact.

## Feature lifecycle

- ACTIVE
- RENAMED
- DISCONTINUED
- ANNOUNCED-NOT-LIVE
- UNVERIFIED

## Financial data rule

A verified feature does not automatically become a verified fee input. Economic applicability still depends on country, category, plan/tier, fulfilment mode, seller status, date and other scope constraints.

## Source rule

Never manufacture an exact source URL from a platform homepage or domain when the underlying source URL is not available in the supplied research artifact. Preserve the research provenance and mark URL-level provenance as unavailable rather than inventing it.

## User-facing wording

Use plain language:

- “Verified from the research source.”
- “Exact fee not publicly published.”
- “This marketplace is researched, but profit calculation is not currently supported.”
- “This value is user-defined.”
- “This information may be stale; verify before making a decision.”
