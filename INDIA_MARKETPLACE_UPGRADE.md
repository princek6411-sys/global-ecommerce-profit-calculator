# India Marketplace Upgrade

## Flipkart
- Uses the public Flipkart seller fee structure for a helper model: fixed fee buckets, collection fee and shipping by packed weight + zone.
- Uses the published zero-commission eligibility signal for products below INR 1,000 and the July 2026 fashion zero-commission announcement.
- Category-specific commission outside eligible cases remains an editable/user-verifiable assumption.
- Official source: https://seller.flipkart.com/fees-and-commission
- Zero-commission model: https://stories.flipkart.com/announcement/flipkart-launches-zero-commission-model-for-products-below-rs-1-000-strengthens-seller-success-with-unmatched-supply-chain-reliability-and-technology
- Fashion update: https://stories.flipkart.com/announcement/flipkart-expands-zero-commission-across-fashion-empowering-sellers-and-accelerating-the-growth-of-indias-fashion-ecosystem

## Meesho
- Marketplace commission is modeled as 0% based on Meesho supplier-facing materials.
- Shipping, returns/RTO, ads and other seller-specific costs remain user-entered because a universal public rate would be misleading.
- Source: https://supplier.meesho.com/sell-online/shirts

## Important
Rates and eligibility can change by seller, category, fulfilment, location and policy updates. The UI therefore keeps assumptions transparent and links to the official source pages.
